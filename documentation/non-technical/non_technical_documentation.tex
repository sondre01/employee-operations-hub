\section{Overview: The Two Working Areas}
% ==========================================

The platform consists of two specialized work areas:

\subsection{1. The Employee Portal (For Everyone)}
Used daily by all team members and client-dedicated engineers:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dual Digital Clocks}: Live clocks for both \textbf{Manila (PHT)} and \textbf{Copenhagen (CEST/CET)}.
    \item \textbf{1-Click Time Clock}: Easy buttons to Clock In, start Lunch, return from Lunch, and Clock Out.
    \item \textbf{Internal Support Tickets}: Fast submission of IT hardware, VPN, HR, and payroll requests.
    \item \textbf{Hardware Custody}: Real-time record of company laptops and gear assigned to you.
    \item \textbf{Policy Compliance}: Read and digitally sign company guidelines and handbooks.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. The Operations Service Desk (For Support Teams)}
Accessible to authorized IT, HR, Finance, and Operations personnel:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Kanban Ticket Board}: Drag-and-drop triage across Open, In Progress, and Resolved columns.
    \item \textbf{Email Auto-Ingestion}: Routing support emails sent to department inboxes.
    \item \textbf{Service Analytics}: Live metrics on resolution velocity, ticket volume, and SLA response times.
    \item \textbf{Access Control (RBAC)}: Safe management of helpdesk permissions for staff members.
\end{itemize}

\section{Step-by-Step Guide for Employees}
% ==========================================

\subsection{Logging In and 1-Click Role Logins}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Open the **Employee Portal** in your web browser.
    \item Enter your corporate email and password (\texttt{Password123!}).
    \item For testing different access tiers, use the **Quick 1-Click Role Logins** bar at the top to switch between DevOps Lead, HR Manager, IT Specialist, or Client Developer with a single click.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Clocking Attendance \& Meal Breaks}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item At the start of your shift, click the blue \textbf{Clock In} button.
    \item When stepping away for your meal break, click \textbf{Start Lunch}.
    \item When returning, click \textbf{End Lunch}.
    \item At the conclusion of your shift, click \textbf{Clock Out}.
    \item Review your past punches anytime on the \textbf{Attendance} ledger.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Submitting an Internal Support Ticket}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Click the \textbf{Tickets} tab in the top navigation.
    \item Click the pink \textbf{Raise Ticket} button.
    \item Select the target department: \textit{IT Support}, \textit{Human Resources}, or \textit{Finance}.
    \item Enter a clear title, description, and urgency level (\textit{Low}, \textit{Medium}, \textit{High}, or \textit{Critical}).
    \item Click \textbf{Submit Ticket}. Your ticket will instantly route to the corresponding team.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Reviewing Assigned Hardware \& Policies}
\begin{itemize}
    \item View your assigned laptop model, serial number, and asset tag right on your dashboard personal card.
    \item Navigate to \textbf{Policies} to read mandatory company guidelines and click \textbf{Acknowledge \& Sign} to digitally confirm your review.
\end{itemize}

\section{Cross-Border Operations (Philippines $\leftrightarrow$ Denmark)}
% ==========================================

\subsection{Understanding the Time Zone Golden Overlap}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Manila Time (UTC+8)} is typically 6 or 7 hours ahead of \textbf{Copenhagen Time (UTC+1/UTC+2)}.
    \item \textbf{Golden Collaboration Window}: \textbf{2:00 PM to 6:00 PM Manila Time} aligns directly with \textbf{7:00 AM to 11:00 AM Copenhagen Time}. Use this window for cross-border meetings and urgent pair programming.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Philippine Statutory \& HR Support}
The HR Helpdesk desk provides streamlined assistance for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{BIR Form 2316}: Annual Certificate of Compensation Payment and Tax Withheld.
    \item \textbf{Statutory Benefits}: Inquiries regarding SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG monthly remittances and loan verifications.
    \item \textbf{Certificate of Employment (COE)}: Rapid issuance for banking, rental, or visa applications.
\end{itemize}
